# Daily Empowerment & Inspiration Kit

A daily habit kit based on the themes of unity, hope, and resilience from the song *Where Rivers Shine* (also titled *Where the Bright Roads Begin*), created via [TextSong.ai](https://textsong.ai/play_music/c3cfdefa-6ef4-45d4-995e-1af744acb24c/en).

## Mission
To share positivity and care with others through daily self-reflection, encouragement scripts, and thought prompts.
